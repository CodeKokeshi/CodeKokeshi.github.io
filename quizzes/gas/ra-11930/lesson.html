Gender and Society - Lesson 10

RA 11930

Anti-Online Sexual Abuse or Exploitation of Children and Anti-Child Sexual Abuse or Exploitation Materials Act of 2022.

Unlawful or Prohibited Acts

Regardless of the consent of the child, it is unlawful to commit prohibited acts through online or offline means, or a combination of both.

Creation or production

Hiring, employing, using, persuading, inducing, extorting, engaging, or coercing a child to participate in OSAEC or CSAEM.

CSAEM production

Producing, directing, manufacturing, facilitating, or creating any form of CSAEM, or participating in the same.

Distribution

Offering, selling, distributing, advertising, promoting, exporting, importing, publishing, transmitting, or broadcasting CSAEM.

Streaming

Producing, directing, creating, hiring, employing, or paying a facilitator to stream or livestream child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Other Prohibited Acts

  • Permitting or influencing the child to engage, participate, or assist in any form of CSAEM.
  • Recruiting, transporting, transferring, harboring, providing, or receiving a child for the purpose of violating the Act.
  • Introducing or matching a child to a foreign national or any person for offenses under the Act.
  • Knowingly benefiting, financially or otherwise, from offenses under the Act.
  • Providing a venue such as dens, private rooms, cubicles, cinemas, houses, private homes, or establishments for prohibited acts.
  • Engaging in luring or grooming of a child, including offline grooming as a prelude to violations.

Digital and Supportive Acts

  • Sexualizing children as objects or subjects of sexual fantasy in online or digital platforms.
  • Engaging in pandering.
  • Willfully subscribing, joining, donating to, or supporting a site that hosts OSAEC or streaming or livestreaming of child sexual abuse and exploitation.
  • Advertising, publishing, printing, broadcasting, or distributing materials that promote OSAEC or child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Possessing any form of CSAEM; possession of three or more CSAEMs is prima facie evidence of intent to sell, distribute, publish, or broadcast.
  • Willfully accessing any form of CSAEM.
  • Conspiring to commit any prohibited act.

Consent, Syndicates, and Large Scale Violations

The consent of the victim is not material or relevant and cannot be used as a defense. A violation is deemed committed by a syndicate if carried out by three or more persons conspiring or confederating. If committed against three or more persons, it is considered a large-scale violation.

Good Samaritan and Safe Harbor

A person responsible for reporting, blocking an internet address, removing a website or domain, taking down shared videos, pictures, or messages, or providing information for investigation or prosecution shall not be held liable if the action is done in good faith, necessary to prevent access or dissemination of CSAEM, and reported within twenty-four hours.

Access, possession, and recording of CSAEM for duties under the Act, reporting to government authorities, legitimate investigation and administration of criminal justice, and legitimate policy, scholarly, and academic purposes with ethical clearance are protected by the safe harbor exception.
