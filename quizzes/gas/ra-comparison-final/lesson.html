RA Comparison

Short guide for deciding whether a sentence points to RA 11313 or another Gender and Society law.

Quick Difference Map

Law Use it when the sentence focuses on... Do not confuse it with RA 11313 because...
RA 11313 Gender-based sexual harassment in streets, public spaces, online spaces, workplaces, schools, or training institutions. It is broader than the old workplace-school sexual harassment law and also covers public and online spaces.
RA 7877 Sexual harassment in employment, education, or training, especially when authority, influence, or moral ascendancy is used. RA 11313 can cover workplace or school harassment too, but RA 7877 is the older authority-based anti-sexual harassment law.
RA 9262 Violence against a wife, former wife, dating or sexual partner, or a woman with whom the offender has a common child, including harm to her child. RA 11313 is about gender-based harassment in spaces; RA 9262 is about intimate or family-related violence against women and children.
RA 8353 Rape as a crime against persons under the Anti-Rape Law. RA 11313 covers harassment and unsafe spaces, while RA 8353 focuses on the crime of rape itself.
RA 11648 Stronger protection against rape, sexual exploitation, and abuse, including the statutory rape age threshold changes. RA 11648 amends rape and child protection rules; RA 11313 is not mainly about statutory rape.
RA 11930 Online sexual abuse or exploitation of children and child sexual abuse or exploitation materials. RA 11313 can cover online gender-based harassment, but RA 11930 is specifically child-focused and involves OSAEC or CSAEM.

Why This RA, Not That RA?

Catcalling in a terminal

Looks like a general sexual harassment issue, but the place and act matter. Because it happens in a public space and involves unwanted gender-based remarks, choose RA 11313.

Supervisor demands a favor

Looks similar to RA 11313 workplace harassment, but if the sentence highlights authority or influence in employment, RA 7877 is the sharper answer.

Partner uses threats at home

Even if the act includes harassment, the relationship controls the answer. Wife, former wife, dating partner, sexual partner, or common-child context points to RA 9262.

Rape or statutory rape clues

If the sentence is about rape itself, choose RA 8353. If it emphasizes the raised age threshold or child protection amendments, choose RA 11648.

Online but child-focused

Online harassment can be RA 11313, but if the sentence includes OSAEC, CSAEM, grooming of a child, streaming exploitation, or child abuse materials, choose RA 11930.

Workplace or school clue

Check what the sentence stresses. General gender-based hostile spaces can be RA 11313; authority-based harassment in work, education, or training can be RA 7877.

Fast Decision Rule

  1. If it says public space, online gender-based harassment, unsafe workplace, or unsafe school environment, think RA 11313 first.
  2. If it says boss, teacher, trainer, authority, or influence, check RA 7877.
  3. If it says wife, former wife, dating partner, sexual partner, common child, or family abode, check RA 9262.
  4. If it says rape, check RA 8353. If the clue is statutory rape age or child protection amendments, check RA 11648.
  5. If it says OSAEC, CSAEM, child sexual exploitation materials, grooming of a child, or online child exploitation, check RA 11930.
