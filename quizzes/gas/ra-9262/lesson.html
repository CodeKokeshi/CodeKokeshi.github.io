RA 9262

Anti-Violence Against Women and their Children Act of 2004.

Violence Against Women and Their Children

Definition

Violence against women and their children refers to any act or series of acts committed by any person against a woman who is his wife, former wife, or against a woman with whom the person has or had a sexual or dating relationship, or with whom he has a common child, or against her child whether legitimate or illegitimate, within or without the family abode.

Result or likely result

The act results in or is likely to result in physical, sexual, or psychological harm or suffering, or economic abuse, including threats, battery, assault, coercion, harassment, or arbitrary deprivation of liberty.

Physical violence

One of the included forms of violence under RA 9262.

Sexual violence

One of the included forms of violence under RA 9262.

Psychological violence

One of the included forms of violence under RA 9262.

Economic abuse

One of the included forms of violence under RA 9262.

Section 5: Acts of Violence

The crime of violence against women and their children is committed through any of the following acts:

  • Causing physical harm to the woman or her child.
  • Threatening to cause the woman or her child physical harm.
  • Attempting to cause the woman or her child physical harm.
  • Placing the woman or her child in fear of imminent physical harm.

Compelling or Restricting Conduct

Violence includes attempting to compel or compelling the woman or her child to engage in conduct they have the right to desist from, or desist from conduct they have the right to engage in. It also includes restricting freedom of movement or conduct by force, threat, harm, or intimidation.

Custody

Threatening to deprive or actually depriving the woman or her child of custody to her or his family.

Support

Depriving or threatening to deprive financial support legally due, or deliberately providing insufficient support.

Legal right

Depriving or threatening to deprive the woman or her child of a legal right.

Work and property

Preventing legitimate profession, occupation, business, or activity, or controlling the victim's own money or properties.

Other Acts Covered

  • Inflicting or threatening to inflict physical harm on oneself to control her actions or decisions.
  • Causing or attempting to cause sexual activity which does not constitute rape, by force, threat of force, physical harm, or intimidation.
  • Engaging in conduct that alarms or causes substantial emotional or psychological distress.
  • Causing mental or emotional anguish, public ridicule, or humiliation.

Acts Causing Emotional or Psychological Distress

Stalking

Stalking or following the woman or her child in public or private places.

Peering or lingering

Peering in the window or lingering outside the residence of the woman or her child.

Entering property

Entering or remaining in the dwelling or on the property against her or his will.

Destroying property

Destroying property and personal belongings or inflicting harm to animals or pets.

Harassment or violence

Engaging in any form of harassment or violence.

Mental or Emotional Anguish

RA 9262 also covers causing mental or emotional anguish, public ridicule, or humiliation to the woman or her child. Examples include repeated verbal and emotional abuse, denial of financial support, and denial of custody of minor children or access to the woman's child or children.
