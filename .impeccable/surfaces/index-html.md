# Portfolio surface

Mode: Experience. Scope: the portfolio home and its Games, Art, Software, Mods, and About views; the timeline and older public portfolio pages inherit the same world.

Audience and job: project browsers and potential collaborators explore real work, led by games and experiments. The footage, playable game, artwork, screenshots, and existing project links are the proof. Keep the site personal, consistent, and usable on phones and desktops.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A world-select surface that lets visitors choose a piece of CodeKokeshi's work, led by the work itself. It replaces the article-like introduction and repeated editorial grids.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep navy and slate stages, warm off-white type, cobalt selection, and amber action marks. Flat game-select panels, a connected project route, large unframed media, and one precise selected state carry the language throughout the site.

**STORY:** A visitor first sees a real game in motion, can play it, then choose another build or browse art, tools, and mods. Brief, candid notes explain the experiments without a formal pitch.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact brand and category rail sits above a wide active game stage. On desktop, a slim selectable project route occupies the left side and the game video dominates the right; title, description, and play action sit directly below it. On mobile, the same stage leads, with a horizontal project selector and thumb-friendly controls. Selecting a project updates the large preview and its details in place; this is the signature interaction.

**FORM:** World Select, the fourth grounded direction in the Experience roll 537595ae. The route and selected stage persist across content types without inventing a game world around the work.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
